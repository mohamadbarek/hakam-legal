1. What Fibra is

Fibra scans product barcodes and gives packaged food and supplements a score from 0 to 100 for the goal you choose. Scores are worked out from the nutrition values on file, which come from Open Food Facts and from people using Fibra.

2. Not medical advice

Scores, verdicts and swaps are general guidance to help you compare products. They are not medical, nutritional or health advice and don't replace a doctor or dietitian. Product data can be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Always check the label, especially for allergens.

3. Fibra Pro

Fibra Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, annual (with a free trial for new subscribers) or monthly, sold by Apple. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when the free trial ends. It renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Apple handles refunds. Scanning stays free and unlimited without Pro.

4. Contributing products

You can add products and correct them. Before your first contribution the app asks you to accept these rules:

  • Copy what's printed on the pack. Photograph the product only, with no people or personal details.
  • Zero tolerance: no offensive, sexual, hateful or abusive words or images, and no spam or links. Fibra has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.
  • Anyone can report a product, and you can hide content from a contributor on your iPhone. Editors review reports, remove content that breaks these rules and ban the accounts that post it.

You keep any rights you have in what you send, and you give Fibra a free, worldwide, permanent licence to store, show, edit and share it in the app and its database. Only send photos and values you have the right to share.

5. Friends

Friends lets you share a summary of your training and food with the people you add, and see theirs. You choose what to share and can stop at any time. The same zero tolerance applies:

  • Use a name that isn't offensive, sexual, hateful or abusive, and don't use someone else's name to mislead. The same goes for the names of workouts, exercises and foods that your friends can see.
  • Add only people who want to be added, and don't use Friends to harass or pressure anyone.
  • You can remove, block or report a friend at any time. Editors review reports, remove profiles that break these rules and ban the accounts behind them.

What friends share with you is meant for you: don't publish it or pass it on without their agreement. The numbers friends share (calories, weights, records) are what they logged themselves. Fibra doesn't check them, and they are not advice for you.

6. Acceptable use

Don't send false product data, try to reach other people's data or accounts, overload or interfere with the service, or copy the database in bulk through the app.

7. Open Food Facts data

Much of the product data comes from Open Food Facts under the Open Database Licence (ODbL), and its photos under CC BY-SA. Product pages credit it.

1. ما هو فيبرا

يمسح فيبرا باركود المنتجات ويمنح الأطعمة المعبّأة والمكمّلات نتيجة من 0 إلى 100 للهدف الذي تختاره. تُحسب النتائج من القيم الغذائية المسجّلة، وهي من Open Food Facts ومن مستخدمي فيبرا.

3. فيبرا Pro

فيبرا Pro اشتراك يتجدّد تلقائياً، سنوي (مع تجربة مجانية للمشتركين الجدد) أو شهري، تبيعه Apple. يُحسب المبلغ على حساب Apple عند تأكيد الشراء أو عند انتهاء التجربة المجانية، ويتجدّد تلقائياً بالسعر نفسه ما لم تُلغه قبل 24 ساعة على الأقل من نهاية الفترة الحالية. يمكنك إدارته أو إلغاؤه من الإعدادات › اسمك › الاشتراكات. تتولّى Apple طلبات الاسترداد. ويبقى المسح مجانياً وغير محدود دون Pro.

4. المساهمة بالمنتجات

يمكنك إضافة المنتجات وتصحيحها. قبل مساهمتك الأولى يطلب منك التطبيق قبول هذه القواعد:

  • انقل ما هو مطبوع على العبوة. صوّر المنتج فقط، دون أشخاص أو تفاصيل شخصية.
  • لا تسامح إطلاقاً: لا كلمات أو صور مسيئة أو جنسية أو تحضّ على الكراهية أو مؤذية، ولا رسائل مزعجة أو روابط. لا يتسامح فيبرا إطلاقاً مع المحتوى المرفوض والمستخدمين المسيئين.
  • يمكن لأي شخص الإبلاغ عن منتج، ويمكنك إخفاء محتوى أي مساهم على جهازك. يراجع المحرّرون البلاغات، ويحذفون المحتوى المخالف، ويحظرون الحسابات التي تنشره.

تحتفظ بأي حقوق لك فيما ترسله، وتمنح فيبرا ترخيصاً مجانياً عالمياً دائماً لحفظه وعرضه وتعديله ومشاركته في التطبيق وقاعدة بياناته. أرسل فقط الصور والقيم التي يحق لك مشاركتها.
